DECISIONS
Home shows the current month in full. If that is fewer than 10 workouts it runs on into the month before until there are 10, and that month’s Earlier row counts only what is left (“October · 6 more workouts”). No workout appears twice. With a workout filter the same rule counts within that workout.
An Earlier month opens in place, in the same list. There is no page of its own for a month.
Better, Lower, Same for a lift is the rule the app already uses: this session’s total (weight × reps) against last time; a lift done only in reps compares total reps. It is worked out once, when the workout is saved, and stored with it, so an old month has marks too. It lives in one function with a comment, so a different rule can replace it later without touching the screens.
A workout with no verdict (a unit change, an unreadable set, no weight in it) shows no marks and the word “Not compared”, never “Same”; its lifts say why. A lift never done before is “First time”, and neither is counted in the workout’s word.
After finishing a workout you land on its Session; Back goes to Workouts.
The whole workout block is one tap target to Session. History always opens on Workouts with All selected; Back from a deeper screen keeps tab, filter, scroll and which months are open.
Edit session is in the options sheet but switched off; the edit screen (row 23, DON08) is parked and not part of the final.
History opens from the Workout hub. The footer has no History tab, and nothing here changes the footer.
The workout’s word is “N better” when any lift is better, else “N lower”, else “Same”, “First time” or “Not compared”. The amber dot means at least one lift is better.
An imported workout has no end time, so it shows no duration.
There is one note, on the workout. A lift has no note of its own.

Decisions, with what was rejected

Big ticking time in a custom card, not Android's small header time. It is the only look where the time reads at a glance. Fallback: the standard card with the time in the header. Rejected: a Live Update (status-bar chip), because a custom card cannot be promoted, and it did not run on this emulator image.
Exercise name shown on the lock screen. It helps between supersets and the notification is public. Rejected: a generic "Next set". One string to change if you disagree.
No buttons. Skip and +30s need a native receiver that acts while the app is closed. TICKET-079's effort stays Medium. A tap opens the app on the active exercise.
0:00. Re-post once: "Go time", static 00:00, not ongoing, so it can be swiped away.
Permission. Sheet once, at the first rest. "Not now" is final. The way back is a settings row. No off switch: declining is the off.
Time colour. Teal while resting. At 0:00: the secondary grey on Android's light card, white on its dark card. The teal is deeper than the in-app accent so it passes contrast on the light card. One colour pair, day and night: Android picked the night value without a re-post on the emulator. A real phone is untested.
Small things. Pulse-bar barbell glyph, one colour (the logo's two ideas, bar and heartbeat; the full-colour logo cannot be a notification icon). The circle is the app accent. Colours come from resources, not hexes in the native code.
